# Mente do Brasil Open Data 2024

`MDB_OPEN_DATA_2024_1` is an immutable local release candidate, not yet publicly released, based on `MDB_ANALYTICAL_2024_2`. It contains Health Region aggregates, 2022-2024 anchors, position-change signals, general health-financing context, disclosure-controlled flows, municipality membership, territorial intelligence, and structural peers.

Use `MANIFEST.json` and `CHECKSUMS.sha256` for integrity. Review `DATA_DICTIONARY.csv`, `LICENSE_DATA.md`, `ATTRIBUTION.md`, and `SOURCES.md` before reuse.

This is territorial intelligence. It is not a patient service directory, individual clinical tool, prevalence estimate, individual-risk measure, direct access measure, quality measure, unmet-need measure, or automatic policy recommendation. AIHs/admissions are not unique patients. LISA HH is not a disease hotspot. Zero beds means zero registered SUS beds under the locked definition.

Financing describes general health-financing context, not mental-health-specific spending. Values are current BRL for each year and are not inflation-adjusted. Missing is not zero.

No individual-level data are included. Exact flows below five and raw small suicide counts are excluded. Geometry is not included because exact-source rights were not sufficiently documented for this release.

Citation: Mente do Brasil. Inteligência territorial em saúde mental no Brasil. Open Data Release MDB_OPEN_DATA_2024_1. Based on MDB_ANALYTICAL_2024_2. 2026.
