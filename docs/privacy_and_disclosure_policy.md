# Privacy and Disclosure Policy

The Open Platform exposes no individual-level data or PII. Public data are aggregated to Health Regions, except municipality-to-region membership. Exact hospitalization-flow cells below five are excluded from downloads and API responses at the disclosure boundary. Raw small suicide counts are not published; rates, flags, and methodological cautions are used instead. Automated scanners cover CSV, Parquet, API fixtures, examples, and documentation.
